Cultivating the Ecosystem

My Suitability & Vision for Sustainability Week 2026

My journey with the Sustainability Department is defined by one core truth: I received so much, and now I want to give back. When I started, I was a blank canvas. The department invested in me, trained me, and gave me the soil to take root. Because they supported my growth, I now have the capacity to support others. I am applying for Sus Week 2026 to use everything they’ve nurtured in me to architect a thriving ecosystem for our community.

Phase 1: The Seed

Started by learning and volunteering. I joined a lot of training sessions organized by the SAP program, absorbing everything like a seed in nourished soil.

Phase 2: The Seedling

Taking part in the project. I broke the surface by becoming the Data Management Coordinator for the Zero Waste project, putting my learning into action.

Phase 3: The Sapling

Ready to architect the ecosystem for others. Having grown from nurtured seed to active participant, I am now ready to help design the environment where others can grow.

My suitability comes down to a simple reality: I have already performed the core responsibilities of this role, just at a smaller scale.

Role Suitability

Activity Content Design 40%

For Sus Week 2025, I didn't just help run a workshop; I co-designed the Sustainable Lip Balm Workshop from scratch—writing the proposal, building the budget, and developing the facilitation materials. I know how to take a sustainability concept and turn it into an interactive participant experience.

Partnership & Cross-team Coordination 30%

As an SIC Volunteer, I navigated the intersection of students, academics, and industry partners by synthesizing their multi-stakeholder discussions into clear documentation. However, my truest test of collaboration was as an SIC Participant. Every session, I was placed in a team of complete strangers. I had to build trust rapidly, navigate diverse perspectives, and produce cohesive outputs. I know how to find synergy among people who don't naturally speak the same language.

Program Consistency & Quality 20%

Creativity needs structure. As the Data Management Coordinator for the Zero Waste Project, I’ve learned the unglamorous but vital skill of standardizing workflows and validating data so that reporting is actually reliable. I will bring this same rigor to reviewing activity content and ensuring alignment across the Sus Week program flow.

Progress Monitoring & Event Support 10%

Having supported RED events with 1,000+ participants and managed VIP logistics, I know what it takes to execute flawlessly on the day. I can document risks and decisions on the fly while keeping the participant experience smooth.

🏆 The Elevator: My recent SIC training equipped me with the "hard framework" skills that elevate this role: Systems Thinking, Theory of Change, and Product Lifecycle Assessment. I don't just intuitively understand sustainability; I can structurally apply it to activity design.

Expectations & Vision

I expect this internship to be a masterclass in Scaling Impact. Thus far, my wins have been relatively contained: a workshop for 40 people, data sets for one project, or coordinating a single stakeholder meeting. Sus Week 2026 is a flagship program reaching over 1,000 people and 10 co-creation partners. From this position, I expect to learn:

🦋

Design for Transformation

I want to see how the Program Lead uses the maturity-based approach (Seeds, Shoots, Deep Roots) to actually measure behaviour change. I expect to learn how to map an activity’s learning outcomes to a long-term Theory of Change, rather than just tracking attendance.

🤝

Mechanics of Co-creation

I’ve coordinated partners before, but "co-creation" implies a deeper level of shared ownership. I expect to learn how the Sustainability Department negotiates, aligns, and builds alongside external NGOs and industry experts so that the program benefits from their expertise without losing its core DNA.

🕸️

Systems-level Orchestration

I expect to gain a "bird’s-eye view" of how content, communications, and operations teams interlock. I want to learn how to monitor the health of a multi-month project, spot bottlenecks before they delay deliverables, and adapt content when partnerships or schedules shift unexpectedly.

From supporting... to architecting.

Ultimately, I expect to walk away from this internship no longer just being someone who supports sustainability initiatives, but someone who knows how to design and architect them from the ground up.
